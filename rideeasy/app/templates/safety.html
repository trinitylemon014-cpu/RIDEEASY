Trust & Safety Architecture

Safety is the foundational contract between RideEasy and our passengers, schools, and driver partners across Uganda.

1. Mandatory Driver Background & Identity Vetting

Every driver on RideEasy must undergo physical verification at our Kampala inspection center. We cross-verify the driver's National Identification Number (NIN) with the National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA), validate valid Driving Permits with the Ministry of Works & Transport, and require an official Certificate of Good Conduct from the Uganda Police Interpol Division.

2. Vehicle Roadworthiness & Speed Limiting

Vehicles operating under RideEasy—from city sedans to 60-passenger school coaches—must hold a valid Certificate of Fitness, active third-party or comprehensive motor insurance, and functioning seatbelts for all passengers. All school buses are equipped with certified speed governors restricted to 80 km/h on expressways and 50 km/h in urban school zones.

3. Real Location Integrity

RideEasy never fabricates fictitious GPS routes or driver locations. When genuine mapping coordinates are captured, trip distances are calculated transparently using road network geometry.

4. Transparent Payment Verification

All mobile money transactions (MTN MoMo and Airtel Money) and card debits are verified server-side with unique audit reference keys. Receipts with itemized tariff breakdowns are generated automatically for every completed trip.
