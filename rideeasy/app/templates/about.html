Corporate Mission · Uganda & East Africa

MOVE. TRAVEL. ARRIVE.

RideEasy is an African transportation and travel engineering platform developed by Emtrixz Technology Company. Founded in Kampala, RideEasy bridges the gap between everyday urban mobility, regulated educational transportation, and international air travel.

Our Origin & Vision

Uganda's transportation landscape has long suffered from fragmented pricing, unvetted operators, and lack of accountability for school children and airport travelers. RideEasy was built from the ground up to solve these structural challenges:

  • Uganda First: Tailored specifically to Kampala and surrounding districts (Wakiso, Entebbe, Mukono, Jinja), integrating local payment infrastructure (MTN Mobile Money & Airtel Money) alongside card processing and exact cash.
  • Institutional Protection: Schools maintain direct command of coaster and bus charters, eliminating unsafe third-party brokering for student travel.
  • Aviation Integrity: Rather than issuing unofficial flight stubs, RideEasy connects travelers directly with authorized, licensed IATA partner agencies who issue authentic PNR bookings with 5-stage automated reminders.
