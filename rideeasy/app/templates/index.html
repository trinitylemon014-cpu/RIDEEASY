Strict Safety Standards

Every ride, driver, and vehicle operating on the RideEasy network undergoes stringent verification in compliance with Ugandan transport regulations:

  • National ID (NIN) & Background Vetting: Verified against official government identification databases.
  • Vehicle Roadworthiness: Mechanical inspections, comprehensive insurance, and valid speed governors.
  • Institutional School Protocols: Schools maintain dedicated dashboards with authorized drivers only.
  • Authorized IATA Travel Agencies: Flight bookings handled exclusively by licensed partner agencies.
